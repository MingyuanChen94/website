Introduction

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Land, Environment, Economics and Policy Institute (LEEP), University of Exeter. I work on environmental and agricultural policy, using econometrics, field experiments and large-scale natural capital modelling to understand how governments can design and target agri-environment policy, and how food systems and firms respond to environmental and market pressures. At LEEP I have worked on policy modelling for Defra and South West Water, and on the Horizon Europe project VISIONARY with partners in eight countries.

Current Projects

NEVm2: Natural Environment Valuation Modelling for Defra

I provide policy-modelling support for Defra's Farming and Countryside Programme. We extend and run LEEP's Natural Environment Valuation (NEV) model suite to test whether targets for woodland creation, carbon and nutrient reductions can be met and are good value for money under different policy levers and budgets.

With Mattia Mancini and Amy Binner, I developed the farmer-uptake model and built its reproducible data pipeline, and I set up parallel processing for the heaviest steps so that scenarios can be rerun quickly when Defra asks for new analysis.

Completed Projects

VISIONARY: Sustainable Food Systems and Resilient Value Chains

A Horizon Europe project with 10 partners in 8 countries (project website). I conducted 41 interviews and co-organised three workshops in England with stakeholders from many backgrounds, including government officials, local councillors, NGOs, businesses, farmers and academics.

I also designed and led a farm-shop field experiment, measuring customer behaviour with a computer-vision pipeline I built in Python. I presented the work on several occasions and reported the project's policy recommendations to EU councillors.

Upstream Thinking: Options and Cost Appraisal for South West Water

With Mattia Mancini and colleagues at LEEP and CREWW, I compared the cost-effectiveness of catchment management with treatment at the point of abstraction across four drinking-water catchments, to support South West Water's WINEP and Water Framework Directive commitments. We worked closely with South West Water staff, including visits to treatment works, and co-authored the final report.
